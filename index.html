How the numbers are done

  1. Primary sourcesFigures come from the SSA, the IRS, Medicare.gov and the Federal Reserve, and every episode lists them. Nothing is estimated from memory.
  2. Assumptions out loudRate of return, inflation, withdrawal rate, claiming age and filing status are stated before any result, so you can hear why your number will differ.
  3. Dated, and remadeAnything that changes yearly is stamped with its year on screen. When the figures change, the episode is remade, not left to go stale.
